How it works

Three steps. One podcast. Zero effort.

Vaani orchestrates data, intelligence, and delivery into a single seamless flow — every morning.

Step 01
🐝

NudgeBee Triggers

At 7 AM, NudgeBee's scheduled workflow fires. It fetches your tasks, upcoming meetings, and curated news from your chosen topics. A human approval gate lets you confirm before anything is sent.

Step 02
🧠

Sarvam AI Composes

Sarvam-105B expands each news headline into rich spoken commentary. The script is translated into your preferred Indian language, then converted to natural-sounding audio using Bulbul v4-Flash TTS.

Step 03
📲

Telegram Delivers

Your personalised 5-minute podcast arrives on Telegram as an audio message. No app to open, no feed to scroll — just tap play and start your day informed. Ask for more anytime in natural language.

Built With

Every sponsor tool is load-bearing.

Remove any one of them and Vaani stops working. That's the Delete Test — and Vaani passes it on all three.

🏴
Flagsmith

Personalisation Engine

Language preference, content block toggles (tasks/news/meetings), and briefing style are all remote config flags. The briefing_format flag runs a live A/B experiment — short vs. full — tracking engagement via briefing_delivered conversion events.

Remote Config · A/B Experiment · Multivariate
🗑 Delete Flagsmith → hardcoded English, no experimentation, no personalisation.
🇮🇳
Sarvam AI

Voice & Intelligence

Sarvam-105B parses natural language requests ("give me 15 mins in Kannada") and expands news headlines into spoken commentary. Sarvam Translate converts the script into 10 Indian languages. Bulbul v4-Flash speaks it aloud with 200+ persona voices.

LLM · Translate · TTS · 10 Indian Languages
🗑 Delete Sarvam → no audio, no multilingual, no intelligence. Just text.
🐝
NudgeBee

Ops & Control Loop

NudgeBee's workflow builder schedules the 7 AM trigger, orchestrates the prepare → approve → send pipeline, and provides the human approval gate before delivery. If Vaani's health endpoint goes down, NudgeBee retries and alerts on-call.

Scheduled Workflow · Approval Gate · Health Monitor
🗑 Delete NudgeBee → no scheduling, no approval gate, no reliability loop.
