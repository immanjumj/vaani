How it works

From schedule to your ears
in three steps.

NudgeBee triggers, Sarvam thinks and speaks, Telegram delivers. Every component is essential — none are decorative.

Step 01

NudgeBee Triggers

At 7 AM IST, NudgeBee's scheduled workflow fires. It calls Vaani's prepare endpoint, assembles your content, and surfaces a human approval gate — so a reviewer confirms the briefing before anything is delivered.

Scheduled Workflow · Approval Gate
Step 02

Sarvam AI Composes

Sarvam-105B reads your Flagsmith-configured profile and parses any natural language override ("15 mins in Kannada"). It expands live news headlines into rich commentary, translates the full script, and feeds it to Bulbul v4-Flash for natural-sounding audio.

LLM · Translate · Bulbul TTS
Step 03

Telegram Delivers

The audio WAV arrives in your Telegram as a voice message. No app to open, no feed to scroll — tap play and start the day. The bot also listens 24/7: type a natural language request any time for an instant on-demand briefing.

Audio Message · NLP Bot · On-demand
Built With

Every integration is
load-bearing.

Remove any one of them and Vaani stops working. That's not integration theatre — that's architecture.

🏴
Flagsmith

Personalisation Engine

Language preference, content toggles (tasks / meetings / news), and briefing style are all remote config flags. The briefing_format flag runs a live A/B experiment — short vs. full — with real conversion tracking via briefing_delivered events.

Remote Config A/B Experiment Conversion Events Multivariate
🗑 Delete Flagsmith → hardcoded English only, no personalisation, no A/B data.
🇮🇳
Sarvam AI

Voice & Intelligence

Sarvam-105B parses natural language requests and expands news headlines into spoken commentary. Sarvam Translate (Mayura) converts the full script into 10 Indian languages. Bulbul v4-Flash generates natural audio across 200+ region-specific voices.

Sarvam-105B LLM Mayura Translate Bulbul v4-Flash TTS 10 Languages
🗑 Delete Sarvam → no audio, no multilingual, no LLM. Just raw text in English.
🐝
NudgeBee

Ops & Control Loop

NudgeBee's workflow builder schedules the 7 AM trigger, orchestrates the prepare → approve → send pipeline, and provides the human-in-the-loop approval gate before delivery. If Vaani's health endpoint goes dark, NudgeBee retries and alerts on-call.

Scheduled Workflow Approval Gate Health Monitor Retry Logic
🗑 Delete NudgeBee → no scheduling, no approval gate, no reliability layer.

Get Started

Start your morning
the right way.

Message the Telegram bot in natural language, or trigger a briefing from the API — your personalised audio is ready in seconds.

vaani — API
# Start NudgeBee simulation
$ curl -X POST localhost:8000/simulate/nudgebee/start
→ {"sim_id": "sim_070000", "status": "awaiting_approval"}
# Trigger a manual briefing
$ curl -X POST localhost:8000/trigger
→ {"status": "triggered", "user_id": "default_user"}
# Or just message the Telegram bot:
🤖 "give me 15 mins of news in Kannada"
→ 🎧 Audio arriving in Telegram...
